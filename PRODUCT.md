# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

FileNest serves desktop users who accumulate documents, downloads, project folders, scans, and media across macOS and Windows. Its primary audience values fast retrieval and automatic organization, but does not want to surrender control of personal or work files to a cloud-only service.

## Product Purpose

FileNest watches user-selected folders, safely waits for new files to stabilize, extracts and indexes their contents, and makes them searchable and discussable in natural language. It combines deterministic organization rules with optional local AI, keeping metadata, embeddings, notes, and conversations on the device by default. The website should make this value immediately understandable and build confidence in the product's privacy boundaries and desktop-native quality.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, protective. FileNest should feel like a dependable desktop companion: technically capable without being theatrical, private without being alarmist, and intelligent without promising magic.

## Anti-references

Avoid neon “AI future” aesthetics, generic cloud-SaaS dashboards, surveillance or fear-based privacy messaging, exaggerated productivity claims, and vague statements about intelligence that are not tied to real product behavior. Do not make the product look like a web-only file storage service.

## Design Principles

1. Show the product doing real work instead of describing abstract AI capabilities.
2. Treat local-first behavior as an understandable product advantage, not a technical footnote.
3. Make every claim traceable to a shipped workflow or documented platform capability.
4. Preserve the calm, native character of the desktop application across marketing surfaces.
5. Be transparent about platform readiness and cloud-provider boundaries.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Maintain readable contrast, keyboard access, semantic structure, visible focus, reduced-motion alternatives, and responsive layouts. Support English and Simplified Chinese without encoding either language directly into application logic.
